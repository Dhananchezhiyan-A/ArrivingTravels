Royal Rajasthan Heritage Circuit
7 Days · 6 Nights · Family / Group Package
Day 1
Arrival in Jaipur

Pickup from Jaipur airport / station. Check-in at heritage property. Evening at Chokhi Dhani for cultural immersion.

Day 2
Jaipur Sightseeing

Amber Fort elephant ride (optional), City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal. Evening at Nahargarh Fort for panoramic sunset views.

Day 3
Jaipur → Jodhpur Drive (335 km)

Scenic drive to the Blue City. Visit Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada cenotaph. Explore the blue lanes at dusk.

Day 4
Jodhpur → Udaipur Drive (270 km)

En-route Ranakpur Jain Temples. Arrive Udaipur — the City of Lakes. Evening boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 5–7
Udaipur & Jaisalmer (Optional Extension)

City Palace, Saheliyon Ki Bari. Optional: extend to Jaisalmer for golden desert dunes, Sam Sand Dunes camel ride.

Nilgiri Mountain Retreat
3 Days · 2 Nights · Hill Station Package

Chennai → Ooty (530 km, ~9 hrs)

Early morning departure in AC luxury vehicle. Hairpin bend drive through Nilgiris. Arrive Ooty by evening. Botanical Garden evening stroll.

Day 2
Ooty → Kodaikanal (120 km)

Ooty Lake, Rose Garden visit. Drive to Kodaikanal via Palani hills. Coaker's Walk at sunset. Kodaikanal Lake paddle boat.

Day 3
Kodaikanal → Chennai Return

Morning at Bryant Park. Drive back to Chennai with a scenic stop at Palani Murugan Temple. Arrive home by evening.
